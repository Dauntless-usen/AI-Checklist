Quick Reference

Never forward these without checking

If it matches one of these, stop and run the routine.

  • Voice notes asking for money
  • Urgent “send it now” messages
  • Quote screenshots with no link
  • Videos with no source or location
  • “Share with 10 people” chains
  • Clips that just confirm what you already believe
  • Anything you cannot trace to an original
  • Intimate or humiliating images of anyone: never create or share

Part Two · Using AI Yourself

The SCOPE check

Run it before you use or share anything AI helped make.

  • S
    SourceCan I verify the facts from reliable sources?
  • C
    ConsentDo I have permission to use someone’s face, voice, work or data?
  • O
    OwnershipHave I disclosed AI help where it is required?
  • P
    PrivacyAm I sharing private or identifying information I do not need to?
  • E
    EffectWho could be harmed if this is wrong, biased or misleading?

For health, money, safety or reputation, add a qualified human. AI can inform a decision. You remain responsible for it.

Myths vs. Reality

What most people get wrong

 

MYTHAn AI detector will tell me.
REALITYDetectors give false positives and false negatives. A score is a signal, not proof.
MYTHIt looks perfect, so it is real.
REALITYPolish proves nothing. Fluent, realistic output can still be false.
MYTHOnly deepfake videos are the danger.
REALITYMisleading AI text can persuade as well as deepfakes (IPIE, May 2026).
MYTHAn earlier upload proves the post is fake.
REALITYIt is a lead, not a verdict. Check the date, place and context too.
MYTHIt sounds like my friend, so it is my friend.
REALITYVoices can be cloned. Call back on a number you already trust.
MYTHContent Credentials or watermarks prove it is true.
REALITYProvenance tells you about a file’s history. It does not prove the caption is true.
Don’t just ask “Does it look real?”
Ask “What evidence supports it?”
